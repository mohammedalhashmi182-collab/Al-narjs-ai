Our story

Karim founded Al-Narjis AI in Riyadh with a simple idea: Saudi business owners deserve AI tools that understand their dialect and their market, and that serve them and their teams instead of replacing them. We started on a single local server, and everything we build today follows the same principle: we build the team you actually need, and the decisions stay with you.

Our mission

AI helps people, it doesn't replace them. Agents handle the repetitive work — replies, follow-ups, drafts — while you and your team approve and decide.

What we do

AI agents & automation

Design

Content & translation

Content writing and translation between Arabic, English and French.

Saudi-built, aligned with Vision 2030
